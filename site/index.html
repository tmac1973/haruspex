Haruspex

Private AI that runs on your computer.

Haruspex is a free, open-source desktop app for research and coding on Linux, Windows and macOS. It downloads a model that fits your hardware and runs it locally. There is no account to create and no telemetry.

Download Source code

What it does

  • Answers questions with web research, citing its sources.
  • Reads and writes files in a folder you choose.
  • Works in your terminal, and helps write and debug code.
  • Reads your email, calendar and contacts when you connect them, including Google Calendar and Google Contacts.
  • Remembers facts you choose to keep, on your device.

Your data

Conversations, files and settings stay on your device. Haruspex runs no servers and receives nothing from you. The privacy policy lists exactly what leaves your computer, and when.
